# KIYO 2026 - ENTRIP Product Concept

[Back to the project hub](https://github.com/limshoon/triple-pendulum/tree/main)

This branch preserves the ENTRIP invention and product brief prepared for **KIYO 2026**.

## Product framing

The KIYO submission translates the research concept into a physical security product composed of three modules:

1. **Triple-pendulum module** - generates irregular mechanical motion influenced by coupled dynamics and real-world perturbations.
2. **Observation module** - captures link and endpoint motion through a camera-based tracking path.
3. **Processing module** - converts observed changes into raw material for evaluation, conditioning, and external output.

The concept brief also explores an enclosure, status indicators, control and data interfaces, and deployment around servers, gateways, embedded systems, and IoT security infrastructure.
